1. Who we are

Naija Opportunity Hub (NOH, we, us or our) operates naijaopportunityhub.com.ng. We help students, graduates and young professionals discover scholarships, jobs, internships, grants, fellowships, training programmes and other opportunities.

We also provide career-support resources, including CV and cover letter tools and an AI-assisted career service.

2. Information we collect

The information processed depends on which parts of the website you choose to use.

Account and sign-in information

If you register for an account, we process information such as your email address and the authentication information needed to sign you in and maintain your account. Our authentication and database infrastructure is provided by Supabase.

Saved opportunities and application tracker

The website may store saved opportunities, bookmarks and application tracker information in your browser's local storage. This allows the relevant features to work when you return to the website.

Clearing your browser storage may remove locally saved information. It may also affect your sign-in session or other website preferences.

Contact messages

If you contact us through our website form, we collect the information you submit. This may include your name, email address, message subject, an opportunity link or reference, and the message itself.

We use this information to review your enquiry, respond where possible, investigate reported problems and maintain a record of communications.

Technical information

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information when you visit the website. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and technical logs. Such information may be used to deliver the website, maintain security, diagnose errors and prevent abuse.

Optional analytics information

If you enable analytics through our cookie settings, Google Analytics may process information about how visitors use the website. This can include pages visited, browser and device information, interactions with the website and approximate location information.

Analytics is optional. You can reject it or withdraw your choice through the cookie settings.

Optional advertising information

If you enable advertising cookies, Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure advertising on eligible pages. Google and its advertising partners may process information according to their own applicable privacy terms and settings.

Advertising scripts are configured to load only on selected website pages and after advertising consent has been given.

3. Career Kit and documents

Information entered into the standard CV and cover letter builder is processed in your browser to create, edit and preview your documents. CV files uploaded for document-related features may also be processed in your browser.

We do not receive the contents of those documents merely because you use the standard builder. However, if you choose an AI feature that requires your document or its text, the relevant information will be transmitted for that request as explained below.

You should review your document before downloading or sharing it. Avoid adding information that is not necessary for the career task you are completing.

4. The AI Career Assistant

Some Career Kit features use an AI Career Assistant to help with tasks such as CV improvement, application writing and career guidance. These features may require you to submit text or document information.

When you choose to use an AI feature, the relevant information you submit is sent from your browser to our server function and then to the AI provider used by that feature to generate a response. The generated result is returned to you.

  • AI requests may be associated with your account and request time for usage monitoring and fair-use limits.
  • The current implementation is designed not to store the contents of your CV or the AI-generated answer in our AI usage records.
  • The AI provider processes submitted information under the terms and policies applicable to the API service and account tier being used.
  • Provider terms may change. Before submitting personal information, review the relevant provider terms and avoid submitting information you do not wish to share with an external AI service.
  • Do not submit passwords, bank details, national identification numbers or other sensitive personal information.
  • AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for your circumstances. Check it carefully before relying on it.

You decide whether to use AI-assisted features. You can use the standard document-building tools without submitting information to the AI assistant.

5. How we use information

We may use information processed through the website to:

  • Provide and maintain website features and user accounts.
  • Save and display information you choose to store.
  • Process requests made through the Career Kit.
  • Apply AI usage limits and help prevent misuse.
  • Respond to enquiries and privacy requests.
  • Review reports about inaccurate, expired or misleading listings.
  • Maintain website security and investigate technical problems.
  • Improve the website where appropriate and permitted.
  • Measure website usage or deliver advertising where you have enabled the relevant optional cookies.

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis for processing, the relevant basis may include providing a service you request, meeting legal obligations, pursuing legitimate interests such as website security, or obtaining your consent for optional processing.

6. Who may process information

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers to operate parts of the website and deliver requested features. Depending on how you use the site, these may include:

  • Supabase: authentication, database services and server-side functions.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, delivery and infrastructure services.
  • Google: AI API processing for AI features, Google Fonts, Google Analytics where enabled, and Google AdSense where advertising consent is enabled.
  • Content delivery providers: delivery of certain code libraries and website resources.

These providers process information according to their applicable service terms and privacy documentation. Some providers may process information in countries outside Nigeria.

We may also disclose information where required by law, where necessary to respond to lawful requests, or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the website.

7. Cookies and browser storage

Essential browser storage

The website uses browser storage and related technologies to support essential functions. Depending on the feature, this may include maintaining sign-in sessions, remembering saved opportunities, storing cookie preferences and supporting website performance.

These technologies are separate from optional analytics and advertising cookies. If you clear browser storage, some website features may stop remembering your preferences or saved information.

Analytics and advertising choices

Our cookie-consent tool allows you to accept all optional categories, reject them, or customise your choices. Analytics and advertising scripts are configured to load only when the corresponding consent has been given.

You can change or withdraw your optional choices at any time by using Cookie settings.

Third-party website resources

Some website resources, including fonts and code libraries, may be delivered by third-party providers. When your browser requests those resources, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address. These resource requests are distinct from the optional analytics and advertising scripts controlled by our consent tool.

8. How long information is kept

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

  • Account information is generally retained while the account remains active and as needed to administer or secure it.
  • Contact messages may be retained for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, follow up on an issue or maintain an appropriate record.
  • AI usage records may be retained as needed for usage monitoring, fair-use limits and service security.
  • Analytics retention is subject to the configuration of the analytics service.
  • Information stored only in your browser remains subject to your browser storage and deletion settings.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, the reason it is processed and any applicable legal obligations.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on your consent.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 provides a framework for protecting personal data in Nigeria. You may find further information through the Nigeria Data Protection Commission .

To request access, correction or deletion of information held by Naija Opportunity Hub, please use our privacy contact form. Select the privacy-related subject and explain your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

If you want to close your account, you can also visit our account deletion page.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information processed through the website. These may include encrypted connections, access controls and database permissions.

No website, online service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use a strong, unique password, keep your sign-in details private and avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information.

11. Children and young users

Naija Opportunity Hub is designed primarily for students, graduates and young professionals. Users under the age of 18 should use the website with appropriate guidance from a parent or guardian.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information for marketing purposes. If you believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

12. External websites

Opportunity listings may link to websites operated by universities, employers, scholarship providers, government agencies and other organisations.

Once you leave Naija Opportunity Hub, the privacy policy and practices of the external website apply. We do not control how those organisations collect or use information. Please review their privacy notices before submitting an application or personal details.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, technology or legal obligations change. The revision date at the beginning of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

Where a significant change affects how personal information is handled, we will take reasonable steps to make the change clear on the website.

14. Contact and privacy requests

If you have a question about this policy, want to make a privacy request or believe your information has been handled incorrectly, please contact us through the Naija Opportunity Hub contact page.

Please do not include passwords, bank details or national identification numbers in your message.
