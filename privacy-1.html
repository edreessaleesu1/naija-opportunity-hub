1. Who we are

Naija Opportunity Hub (“NOH”, “we”, “us”) is a website at naijaopportunityhub.com.ng that helps Nigerian students and graduates find scholarships, jobs, internships, grants, fellowships and training, and provides career tools such as a CV builder.

2. Information we collect

Account information

If you create an account, we process your email address and the sign-in details needed to keep your account secure. Accounts are provided through Supabase, our authentication and database provider.

Saved opportunities and tracker

Opportunities you save and applications you track are stored in your browser on your device.

Career Kit documents

What you type into the CV and cover letter builder, and any CV file you upload, is processed in your browser to build and preview your document. We do not receive it unless you use an AI feature (see section 3).

Messages you send us

If you use our contact form, we store your name, email address, the subject, any link you add and your message, so we can reply.

Technical information

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and our other service providers may process technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

3. The AI Career Assistant

The AI Career Assistant is available to signed-in members. When you tap an AI button, the text you have entered or uploaded (for example your CV text, job title or letter details) is sent securely from your browser to our server function and then to Google’s Gemini API to generate the result, which is returned to you.

  • We record that a request was made (your account ID and the time) so we can apply fair-use limits. We do not store the content of your CV or the AI’s answer.
  • We currently use Google’s free tier. Under Google’s terms for that tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Do not include anything you are not comfortable sharing on those terms.
  • Please do not include passwords, bank details, national ID numbers or other sensitive information in anything you submit.
  • AI output can be wrong or incomplete. Check it before you use it.

4. How we use information

  • To provide and secure the site, your account and the Career Kit tools.
  • To reply to your messages and fix reported problems with listings.
  • To apply usage limits and prevent misuse of the AI feature.
  • To maintain and improve the site.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on the performance of our service to you, our legitimate interest in running and securing the site, and your consent where you choose to use optional features.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We use trusted service providers who process data for us:

  • Supabase – sign-in, database and server functions.
  • Cloudflare – website hosting and delivery.
  • Google – the Gemini API for AI features, and Google Fonts for typefaces.
  • Content delivery networks – to load code libraries used by the site.

These providers may process data outside Nigeria. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of users and the site.

Links to other sites. Opportunity pages link to official websites run by other organisations. We do not control them, and their privacy practices apply once you leave NOH.

6. Cookies and device storage

We use your browser’s local storage and a service worker to keep you signed in, remember saved opportunities, and make the site load faster and work better offline. These are needed for the site to work. You can clear them in your browser settings, which may sign you out and remove saved items.

7. How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is active. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the request. AI usage records are kept for a short period for fair-use limits. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

8. Your rights and choices

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law, you may have the right to ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To make a request, use our contact form and choose “Privacy request”.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections, access controls and restricted database permissions. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

The site is intended for people aged 18 and over, or younger users with the guidance of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing purposes.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. If we make a significant change, we will make it clear on the site.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Please use the contact form.
