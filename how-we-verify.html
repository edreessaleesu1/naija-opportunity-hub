Our editorial standards

How we check opportunities

We want every visitor to understand where opportunity information comes from, what we check before publication and what our verification label does — and does not — mean.

1. What our Verified label means

A Verified label means that we checked an opportunity against an official source on the verification date displayed on its listing. We confirmed that the opportunity appeared to exist, that its key published details were consistent with the source and that its application link worked when we checked it.

The verification date tells you when we last carried out those checks. It does not mean that the information has been monitored continuously or that the organisation has made no changes since that date.

If a listing is marked Not verified yet, our verification checks have not been completed. Treat the information as unverified and consult the organisation's official website before taking action.

2. Where we find opportunities

We look for opportunities through sources that can be traced to the organisations responsible for offering them. These may include:

  • Official employer and organisation websites.
  • Government ministry, agency and public institution websites.
  • University and other recognised education institution websites.
  • Official scholarship, fellowship and grant programme websites.
  • Official training, internship and recruitment announcements.
  • Application portals identified by the organisation offering the opportunity.

News reports, opportunity directories, social media posts and third-party websites may help us discover an announcement. However, where possible, we use the original organisation's announcement or official application channel to confirm the information before marking a listing as verified.

3. What we check before verification

Our checks depend on the type of opportunity and the information published by its organiser. We aim to review the following details:

Organisation and programme

We check whether the named organisation and opportunity can be identified through an official source. We look for a credible connection between the opportunity and the organisation offering it.

Eligibility and intended applicants

We compare the stated qualifications, applicant group, location, age requirements where applicable and other published eligibility conditions.

Deadline and benefits

We check the published application deadline and the benefits described by the organiser, such as funding, salary, training, accommodation or other programme support. We avoid presenting benefits as guaranteed where the official announcement makes them conditional.

Application route

We check whether the application link leads to the official organisation or a clearly identified application platform. Where possible, we avoid directing applicants to unrelated intermediaries.

Accuracy and duplication

We look for expired, duplicated, incomplete or inconsistent information. We also check whether an announcement has been withdrawn or replaced by a newer version.

Safety concerns

We look for suspicious instructions, misleading promises, impersonation and requests for payment to unofficial parties. A listing that raises unresolved concerns may be withheld from publication or marked for further review.

Our checks take place at a particular time. We cannot guarantee that every organiser will keep its website, deadline, eligibility rules or application process unchanged afterwards.

4. What verification cannot guarantee

A Verified label is an indication of the checks we carried out. It is not a guarantee of the organisation's future actions or the outcome of an application.

Verification does not mean that:

  • You will be selected, employed, admitted or awarded funding.
  • The organisation will keep the opportunity open until its stated deadline.
  • Eligibility rules, benefits or application instructions cannot change.
  • Naija Opportunity Hub is the employer, funder, university, sponsor or programme organiser.
  • We have independently audited the organisation or guaranteed its services.
  • Every detail has been independently confirmed beyond the checks described on the listing.

Always read the latest official announcement and follow the organisation's own instructions before submitting an application. If information on our website differs from the current official announcement, rely on the official announcement and tell us about the difference.

5. Application fees and staying safe

Some legitimate institutions or programmes may charge official application, examination or processing fees. A fee should not be assumed to be genuine simply because it appears in a message, advertisement or website.

Before making a payment or sharing personal information:

  • Confirm any required fee directly through the organisation's official website or verified contact channel.
  • Check that the payment recipient and payment instructions match information published by the organisation.
  • Do not pay an unofficial agent or intermediary who promises guaranteed admission, employment, a scholarship or selection.
  • Check website addresses carefully for misspellings and look-alike domains.
  • Never disclose your account password, one-time verification code or bank card PIN to someone claiming to help with an application.
  • Be cautious about requests for sensitive personal or financial information that are not clearly required by the official process.
  • Keep copies of official announcements and payment receipts where a legitimate fee applies.

Naija Opportunity Hub does not collect application fees on behalf of the organisations featured in its opportunity listings. If someone claims to represent us and requests money in exchange for access to an opportunity or a guaranteed outcome, contact us through our official contact page.

6. Corrections, updates and expired listings

Opportunity information can change. A deadline may be extended, an application portal may close, eligibility may be revised or an organisation may withdraw a programme.

When we receive a credible report or identify a problem ourselves, we may investigate and take appropriate action. This may include:

  • Correcting inaccurate information.
  • Updating a deadline or application link after checking the new source.
  • Adding a clarification or update to the listing.
  • Marking an opportunity as expired or closed.
  • Temporarily removing a listing while a concern is investigated.
  • Withdrawing a listing where its accuracy or legitimacy cannot be established.

We aim to review correction reports as soon as reasonably possible. Sending a report does not mean that a change has already been made. We may need additional information or time to check the issue.

7. Editorial independence

Naija Opportunity Hub is an independent information platform. We are not an employer, scholarship provider, government agency or application decision-maker unless a specific relationship is clearly disclosed.

Opportunity listings are published to inform visitors. A listing does not mean that we endorse every service, decision or activity of the organisation offering it.

Advertising, where displayed, is separate from our editorial decisions. Advertisements are not the same as verified opportunity listings. Payment for advertising does not, by itself, establish that an opportunity has passed our verification checks.

We aim to present opportunity information accurately and make corrections when credible new information becomes available.

8. Images and supporting materials

Images may be used to help visitors recognise an organisation, programme or opportunity. Where suitable materials are available, we prefer images and logos from official organisational sources or other sources that can be appropriately identified.

A photograph, logo or illustration is not proof that an opportunity is genuine. The presence of an organisation's logo on an image does not mean that the organisation has endorsed Naija Opportunity Hub or approved the specific listing.

We may use illustrative images where an official image is not available. Such images should not be interpreted as official programme materials or as photographs of actual participants unless that is clearly established.

9. Report an inaccurate or suspicious listing

If you find an inaccurate deadline, broken application link, outdated announcement, duplicate listing or suspicious request, please let us know.

To help us investigate, include:

  • The title or link of the opportunity.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • The official source or announcement, if available.
  • A screenshot or other supporting information, where useful.

You can submit a report through our contact page. Please do not send passwords, bank card PINs or one-time verification codes.

We believe reliable information is an important starting point for finding opportunities. Your feedback helps us identify outdated details and improve the information we publish.
