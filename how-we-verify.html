Our editorial standards

How we check opportunities

We want visitors to understand where opportunity information comes from, what our checks cover and what a verification label does — and does not — mean.

Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

Our promise: We aim to make opportunity information clearer and easier to check. We use official sources wherever possible, identify information that has not been verified and encourage every applicant to confirm the latest details with the organisation offering the opportunity.

1. What our Verified label means

A Verified label means that, on the date displayed on the listing, we checked the opportunity against an official source and confirmed that the opportunity appeared to exist, its key published details were consistent with that source, and the application link was working when we checked it.

The verification date tells you when we last carried out those checks. It is not a promise that the organisation has made no changes since that date.

If a listing says Not verified yet, our verification checks have not been completed. Treat the information as unverified and consult the organisation's official website before taking action.

2. Where we find opportunities

We look for opportunities through sources such as:

  • Official employer and organisation websites.
  • Government ministry, agency and public institution websites.
  • University and education institution announcements.
  • Official scholarship, fellowship and grant programme websites.
  • Official programme announcements and application portals.

News reports, opportunity directories, social media posts and other third-party sources may help us discover an announcement. Where possible, we use the original organisation's announcement to confirm the information before marking a listing as verified.

3. What we check before verification

Organisation and programme

We check whether the named organisation and opportunity can be identified through an official source.

Eligibility and audience

We compare the stated qualifications, location, applicant group and other published eligibility conditions.

Deadline and benefits

We check the published deadline and any stated funding, salary, support or programme benefits.

Application route

We check whether the application link leads to the official organisation or a clearly identified application platform.

Accuracy and duplication

We look for expired, duplicated, incomplete or inconsistent information before publication.

Safety concerns

We look for suspicious instructions, misleading claims and requests for payment to unofficial parties.

Important: We do not claim that every listing is continuously monitored. Our checks take place at a particular time, and information may change afterwards.

4. What verification does not guarantee

A Verified label does not mean that:

  • You will be selected, employed, admitted or awarded funding.
  • The organisation will keep the opportunity open until its stated deadline.
  • Eligibility rules, benefits or application instructions cannot change.
  • Naija Opportunity Hub is the employer, funder, university, sponsor or programme organiser.
  • We have independently audited the organisation or guaranteed the outcome of its programme.

Always read the latest official announcement and follow the organisation's own instructions before submitting an application.

5. Application fees and staying safe

Some legitimate institutions or programmes may charge official application, examination or processing fees. A fee should never be assumed to be genuine simply because it appears in a message or on a website.

  • Confirm any required fee directly through the organisation's official website or verified contact channel.
  • Do not pay an unofficial agent or intermediary who promises guaranteed admission, employment, a scholarship or selection.
  • Check website addresses carefully for misspellings and look-alike domains.
  • Never disclose your account password, one-time verification code or bank card PIN to someone claiming to help with an application.
  • Be cautious about requests for sensitive personal or financial information that are not clearly required by the official process.
  • Keep copies of official announcements and payment receipts where a legitimate fee applies.

6. Corrections, updates and expired listings

Opportunity information can change. A deadline may be extended, an application portal may close, eligibility may be revised or an organisation may withdraw a programme.

If we receive a credible report or identify a problem, we may investigate, correct the listing, add an update, mark it as expired, temporarily remove it or withdraw it.

We aim to review correction reports as soon as reasonably possible. Sending a report does not mean a change has already been made.

To report an issue, use the contact form. Include the opportunity link, describe the problem and, where possible, provide the official source that supports the correction.

7. Editorial independence

Naija Opportunity Hub is an independent information platform. We are not an employer, scholarship provider, government agency or application decision-maker unless a specific relationship is clearly disclosed.

Opportunity listings are published to inform visitors. A listing does not mean that we endorse the organisation or guarantee its services or decisions.

Advertising, where displayed, is separate from our editorial decisions. Advertisements are not verified opportunity listings.

8. Help us improve the information

If you find an inaccurate deadline, broken link, outdated announcement or suspicious listing, please let us know. Reports from visitors help us identify information that may need another check.
