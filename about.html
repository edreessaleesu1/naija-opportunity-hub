About us

Opportunity should be easier to find.

Naija Opportunity Hub helps Nigerian students, graduates and early-career professionals discover opportunities and prepare stronger applications through practical career resources.

Finding a scholarship, a first job, an internship or a useful training programme can take time. Information is often scattered across different websites, and it is not always easy to tell whether the details are current.

We bring opportunity information and application support together in one place. Our aim is to make the search clearer, reduce unnecessary confusion and help visitors take informed next steps.

Our mission

Our mission is to make useful opportunities more accessible to Nigerian students, graduates and young professionals, regardless of where they are starting from.

We organise information about scholarships, jobs, internships, grants, fellowships, training programmes, volunteering and other development opportunities. We also provide resources to help visitors prepare their applications.

What we do

  • Opportunity discovery: We publish information about local and international opportunities relevant to our audience.
  • Career preparation: Our Career Kit provides CV templates and tools to support the preparation of career documents and applications.
  • Practical guidance: We publish guides intended to help visitors understand application processes and make better-informed decisions.
  • Personal organisation: Website features such as bookmarks and application tracking can help users keep their opportunity search organised.

What guides our work

  1. Accuracy We aim to check important details against official organisation sources and make application links clear.
  2. Transparency We distinguish our independent information service from the organisations that provide the opportunities.
  3. Practical value Our listings, guides and tools should help visitors take useful next steps, not simply add to online information.
  4. Fair access We aim to present relevant opportunities clearly so that people can assess them against their own circumstances.

Our editorial standards

Naija Opportunity Hub is an independent information platform. We are not an employer, scholarship provider, university, government agency or application decision-maker.

We look for opportunities through official programme, employer, university, foundation and government sources. Where possible, listings link directly to the organisation's own announcement or application page.

Before publication, we aim to check the organisation, opportunity details, stated eligibility, deadline, location and application instructions. We also look for expired, duplicated or inconsistent information.

Our checks are made at a point in time. A verification date does not guarantee that an organisation has not changed its requirements after our review. Applicants should always confirm the latest information on the official source before applying.

Read more about our process on the How We Verify Opportunities page.

Corrections and feedback

Opportunity details can change. If you notice an incorrect deadline, broken application link, changed eligibility rule or suspicious listing, please report it to us.

Include the relevant page link and, where possible, the official source showing the correction. We review reports and update, close or remove listings when appropriate.

Use our Contact page to reach the team.

Our independence

The presence of an organisation or opportunity on this website does not automatically mean that the organisation endorses Naija Opportunity Hub or that we are affiliated with it.

Advertising, where displayed, is separate from our editorial listings. Advertising does not determine which opportunities we publish or how we describe them.
