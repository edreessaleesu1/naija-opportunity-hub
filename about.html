ABOUT US

Opportunity should be easier to find.

Naija Opportunity Hub helps Nigerian students, graduates and early-career professionals discover opportunities and prepare stronger applications with practical career resources.

OUR PURPOSE

Helping people move from discovery to possibility.

Finding a scholarship, a first job, an internship or a useful training programme can take time. Information may be scattered across many websites, and it can be difficult to tell which details are current. We bring opportunity information and application support together in one place, with a focus on clarity, usefulness and responsible sourcing.

WHAT GUIDES OUR WORK

The standards we aim to uphold

Our editorial and verification standards

Naija Opportunity Hub is an independent information platform. We are not an employer, scholarship provider, government agency or application decision-maker. Unless a page clearly says otherwise, opportunities are offered and administered by the organisations named in each listing.

How we source opportunities

We look for opportunities from official programme, employer, university, foundation and government sources. Where possible, a listing links directly to the organisation's own announcement or application page. Third-party pages may help us discover an opportunity, but they are not a substitute for checking the original source.

What we check

  • The identity of the organisation or programme and whether the announcement can be found through an official source.
  • The opportunity type, stated eligibility, location or audience, benefits where published, deadline and application instructions.
  • Whether the application link leads to an official or clearly identified application destination.
  • Whether a listing appears expired, duplicated, incomplete or inconsistent with the source information.

Our checks are made at a point in time. A verification date is not a guarantee that an organiser has not changed its requirements afterwards. Always confirm the latest details on the official opportunity page before applying.

Read the full process on our How We Check Opportunities page.

Corrections, updates and expired listings

Opportunity details can change after publication. If you notice an incorrect deadline, broken application link, changed eligibility rule or suspicious listing, please tell us through our contact form. Include the page link and, if possible, the official source showing the correction. We review reports and update, close or remove listings when appropriate.

Our guides and career resources

Our guides are published to provide practical information for students, graduates and job seekers. They are general educational resources, not a promise of admission, employment, funding or selection. Requirements differ between organisations, so applicants should follow the instructions of the specific programme or employer.

The Career Kit can help users prepare CVs and application documents. Users should check all generated or drafted content for accuracy and ensure that their applications represent their real qualifications and experience.

Editorial responsibility

Our content is maintained by the Naija Opportunity Hub editorial team. We aim to review information when updates are reported or when we revisit a listing or guide. We do not publish a personal editor's name where an individual author or reviewer has not been designated.

Independence and advertising

Listings are informational and do not mean that Naija Opportunity Hub endorses an organisation or guarantees an opportunity. Advertising, where displayed, is separate from editorial listings and does not determine which opportunities or guidance we publish.
