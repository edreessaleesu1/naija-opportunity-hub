Before you contact us

Reporting an opportunity

If you find a wrong deadline, broken application link, outdated information or suspicious listing, select the reporting option and include the page link. Where possible, share the official source that supports your report.

Career resources

For CV preparation, cover letters and application guidance, explore our Career Kit and career guides.

Partnerships and opportunity submissions

Organisations may contact us with information about programmes and opportunities. Submissions are subject to our sourcing and verification standards. Sending information does not guarantee publication.

Privacy requests

For questions about personal information, select "Privacy request". Please do not include passwords, bank details or unnecessary identity documents in your message. Read our Privacy Policy.

Staying safe

  • We will never ask you to disclose your account password or one-time verification code.
  • Confirm any application fee directly with the official organisation offering the opportunity.
  • Do not pay unofficial agents who promise guaranteed admission, employment or selection.
