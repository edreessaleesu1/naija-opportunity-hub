How to Build a Professional CV Online with the Career Kit

A well-designed CV makes a strong first impression, but many people do not have access to design software or know where to start. The Naija Opportunity Hub Career Kit lets you build one in your browser, from a phone or a computer, and download it as a PDF. This guide shows you how to get the best result.

Step 1: Choose the right group of templates

Open the CV builder. Templates are grouped by purpose so that you can pick quickly:

  • Everyday: classic, graduate, Nigerian graduate, plain ATS-friendly and one-page styles for most job applications.
  • Professional: modern designs with colour banners, sidebars, timelines and cards, many with space for a photo.
  • Study and research: a scholarship CV, a full academic CV with sections for research, teaching, publications and grants, and a Europass-style layout.

Choose a calm, readable design for conservative employers such as banks and law firms. Choose a bolder one for creative and technology roles. If a company uses online screening software, the plain ATS-friendly template is the safest option.

Step 2: Fill in your details

Each template shows a short hint explaining what it is best for and gives example text in each box. Enter your contact details, then your profile, education, experience and skills. For work experience, write the job title on one line, the organisation and dates on the next, and use lines starting with a dash for achievements. Include numbers wherever you can. Our CV format guide explains what to include.

Step 3: Add a photo if you want one

Some templates have an optional photo box. A photo is not required by most employers, so add one only if the job advertisement asks for it or the culture of your industry expects it. Your photo stays on your own device and is not uploaded to our servers. Use a clear, plain image with good light.

Step 4: Pick a colour theme

Choose from several colour themes, such as green, navy, burgundy, teal, charcoal and gold. Darker, simple colours look more formal. Keep to one colour so the page stays easy to read.

Step 5: Preview, check and download

  1. Click Preview and read your CV from top to bottom.
  2. Check spelling, dates and contact details. Wrong phone numbers are a common and costly mistake.
  3. Download the PDF and open it on your phone or computer to confirm the layout is correct.
  4. Name the file clearly, for example "Your-Name-CV.pdf".

Step 6: Tailor it for every application

Change the profile and highlight the experience that best matches the job description. You can keep a general version and edit it each time you apply. Pair your CV with a cover letter from our cover letter tool.

Privacy

Your CV details are used to build your document in your browser. Read our privacy policy for how any information is handled, and do not include sensitive information such as passwords or card numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Is the CV builder free?

Yes, you can build and download your CV for free.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. A photo you add stays on your own device.

Which template is safest for online job portals?

The plain ATS-friendly template, because it uses a simple layout that screening software can read.
