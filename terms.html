4. Application safety and fees

Your safety matters. Before submitting personal documents, paying an application fee or sharing financial information, verify the request directly with the organisation offering the opportunity.

  • Use the official application website or a contact channel independently confirmed by the organisation.
  • Be cautious of individuals who claim they can guarantee admission, employment, a scholarship or selection in exchange for payment.
  • Do not send money to personal accounts or unofficial agents simply because they refer to a listing on NOH.
  • Some legitimate application processes may have official fees. Check the fee directly with the organisation and confirm its purpose, amount and payment instructions before paying.
  • Never share passwords, one-time verification codes or unnecessary sensitive information with someone claiming to represent an opportunity provider.

NOH does not collect application fees on behalf of the organisations whose opportunities we list. Unless a service is expressly described as an NOH service, payment requests from third parties are not payments to NOH.
