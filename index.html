Stay safe online

Apply Smart. Avoid Opportunity Scams.

A few simple checks can help protect your money and personal information.

✓

Use Official Sources

Confirm opportunities on the employer's, university's or funder's official website before applying.

✓

Be Cautious with Fees

Do not send money to individuals promising guaranteed jobs, scholarships, grants or selection.

✓

Protect Your Data

Share sensitive identity or banking information only when necessary and through a verified channel.
