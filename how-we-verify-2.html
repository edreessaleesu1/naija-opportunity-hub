How we check opportunities

What the Verified label means, and how to protect yourself from scams.

What "Verified" means

A ✓ Verified label means that on the date shown, we opened the official source of the opportunity (the organisation's own website or application page) and checked that the opportunity exists, that the main details match, and that the application link works.

The date is the day we last checked. If a listing shows Not verified yet, we have not completed these checks. Confirm the details on the official website before you apply.

What we check

  • The opportunity is published on the organisation's official website or an official channel.
  • The title, deadline and eligibility match the official announcement.
  • The application link leads to the organisation's own page.
  • Nothing in the listing asks applicants to pay a fee to us or to an agent.

What "Verified" does not mean

It is not a guarantee that you will be selected, and deadlines or requirements can change after we check. Always read the official announcement and apply through the official link. Naija Opportunity Hub is not the employer, school or funder.

Staying safe

  • Genuine scholarships, jobs and grants do not ask you to pay to be considered.
  • Check that the web address belongs to the organisation. Be careful with look-alike addresses.
  • Never share your password, bank card details or ID numbers in an application.
  • Be cautious of anyone who contacts you by WhatsApp or social media promising a guaranteed place.

Found a problem?

If a listing is wrong, out of date or looks suspicious, tell us. Use the "Report a problem" link on the listing or the contact page, and we will review it.
